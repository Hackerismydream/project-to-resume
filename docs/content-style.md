# Content Style

- Write in clear Chinese for the default resume output; keep necessary engineering terms in their common form.
- Lead with the task object, failure, decision, and observable behavior—not a technology list.
- Use one main conclusion per bullet.
- Prefer concrete verbs such as 负责、设计、实现、重构、定位、验证.
- Reserve 主导、牵头、上线、支撑、提升 for evidence-backed ownership or outcomes.
- Avoid enterprise-grade, industrial-grade, massive scale, significant improvement, and similar unbounded marketing language.
- Keep internal repository paths and class names out of the default resume unless they are necessary for understanding.
- Ask follow-up questions in plain language, no more than three, and only when the answers can materially improve positioning, ownership, or a number.
- Do not append method reports, evidence ledgers, or disclaimers after a complete default draft.
