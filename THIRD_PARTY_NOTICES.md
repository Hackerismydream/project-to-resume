# Third-party notices and methodology provenance

This skill uses original wording and implementation. Its design was informed by:

- [Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills/tree/74ae19e7c62b0516d1c298328e5544976c12da5d),
  MIT licensed. The truthful-tailoring, bullet-structure, case-study, and version
  management ideas were studied. Guidance that estimates missing metrics or
  requires a number in every bullet was deliberately not adopted.
- [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/6acc160e4e0cd062dbbbd7a1b26ae92855edf07e),
  MIT licensed. The skill applies concise branch routing, progressive disclosure,
  leading concepts, and checkable completion criteria without copying templates.
- A user-supplied six-step project-to-resume method: scene, failure, observable
  behavior, baseline, result, and evidence boundary.
- A local `deep-investigate` workflow informed the optional independent-review
  branch. No local source text or executable component is redistributed, and the
  skill has no runtime dependency on it.

The three README examples are analyses of public repositories at pinned commits.
They do not copy project code and do not assert that this skill's author
contributed to those projects.
